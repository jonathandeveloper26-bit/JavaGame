# Level 5 - The long swim
# Owner: Jonathan (X4, taken over from Xaryab). The hardest level.
#
# Designed for a rank 3 frog (hop 0.14, can swim), the usual rank by now. The river is
# about 0.51 wide and the bag holds only two twigs.
#
# Pad P1 is one long hop from the bank. Pad P2 lies beyond hop range, and a reed clump
# shadows it: a twig thrown straight above P2 sends a ripple the reed blocks. Throw from
# the side instead, and the ripple pushes P2 into reach a turn later. From P2 there are
# no more pads: swim the rest, past a fly, and climb out onto the far bank. Swimming
# costs four times the energy of a walk.
#
# Balancing note: until the predators are in, a rank 3 frog can also swim the whole river
# for about 22 of its 28 energy, skipping the pad puzzle. The fish should make that too
# dangerous; check at the balancing session.
#
# One way to win (LevelsTest plays it): throw a twig at (0.419, 0.126) and walk to the
# edge; hop to P1; hop to P2 once it is pushed; swim to (0.52, 0.26), (0.52, 0.165) and
# out at (0.52, 0.075).
