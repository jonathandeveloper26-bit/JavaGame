RIPPLE POND - HOW TO PLAY
Get the frog from the bank at the bottom to the far bank at the top.
Each turn you may throw one object and make one move. Then the river reacts:
  1. Your object lands where you click on open water and starts a ripple.
  2. Every ripple grows.  3. Floating things its ring reaches are pushed away.
  4. The frog makes its move.  5. Predators move and attack.
Walk on a bank or along the object you stand on. The cheapest move.
Hop from one bank or floating object to another. Twice the energy of a walk.
Swim through open water once you reach rank 3. Four times the energy of a walk.
Longer moves cost more energy. A move you cannot pay for is refused.
Touch food to get health and energy back. Touch an icon to add an object to your bag.
Reeds block ripples and pushes. A ripple wakes a sleeping turtle and moves it.
Win: reach the far bank. Lose: run out of health, energy or turns.
Winning earns XP. Higher ranks hop further and have more health and energy.
Reset restarts the level and keeps your XP.
